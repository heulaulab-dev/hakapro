Brand identity · Logo usage · v1.0

HAKA PRO is a research terminal for Indonesian equities. The logo should read like the product: precise, calm and built on a grid. These rules keep it that way across the app, decks, social and print.

Read the market. Follow the flow.Updated 04 Oct 2026Typeface · Plus Jakarta Sans
01 · Anatomy

A symbol and a wordmark, locked together

The symbolFour solid columns on a 400-unit grid. The negative space between them forms the H. The shape is 180° rotationally symmetric, so it reads the same way upside down, like a balanced order book.
The wordmarkHAKA is set in Bold 700 and PRO in Medium 500, both in Plus Jakarta Sans with +10% tracking. The weight contrast puts the emphasis on the brand name and treats PRO as the tier. All text is outlined in the files.
  1. Grid: stems 52u · gaps 84u · core column 128u · core split 88u · corner radius 24u · stem curve 52×120u ellipse.
  2. Lockup: the cap height of the wordmark is 36% of the symbol height. The gap from symbol to wordmark is 30% of the symbol height, and the wordmark is centred vertically on its cap height.
  3. Never rebuild: always use the master files. Do not retype the wordmark or redraw the symbol.
02 · Variants

Pick the lockup by the space you have

Use the horizontal lockup by default. Use the stacked lockup when the space is square or tall. Use the symbol alone only where HAKA PRO is already named nearby, such as an app icon, favicon, avatar or the app's top bar.

03 · Color

Deep sea to shallow water

The symbol always carries the brand gradient at 135°, from the top-left to the bottom-right corner, across the whole mark rather than each column. The wordmark always uses flat colors.

04 · Clear space

Keep x clear on every side

x = 0.39 × the symbol height, which is the height of one core block of the symbol. Text, other logos, UI chrome and the edge of the canvas stay outside this zone. In the app header, x can be reduced to 0.25 if the header's own padding already provides the space.

05 · Minimum size

Below these sizes, switch variant

At small sizes the gaps in the symbol close up and the PRO strokes get thin. If the logo has to go smaller than the limit, move down to the next variant instead of shrinking it.

Below 24 px, use favicon.svg. It sits on a solid Night tile and has a brighter gradient start so it stays visible at 16 px.

06 · Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans

This typeface was designed by Tokotype in Jakarta, so the brand's own type comes from the market it covers. It's free under the SIL Open Font License on Google Fonts. Use it for headings and UI that sit next to the logo so the wordmark and the product read as one family.

07 · Misuse

Don't

Stretch or squashAlways scale proportionally. Lock the aspect ratio.
Rotate or tiltThe symbol's balance depends on it staying upright.
Recolor the gradientNo seasonal or "green for bullish" recolors.
Add effectsNo shadows, glows, bevels, outlines or the old glass facets.
Haka Pro
Retype the wordmarkDon't use script or serif fonts, or mixed-case "Haka Pro". Use the outlined files.
Busy or mid-tone groundsUse mono-white on photos and mid-tones.
  • Don't put PRO in a badge or pill, or separate it from HAKA. It's part of the name, not a sticker.
  • Don't use the old HeulaTrade marks or the earlier script-H logo alongside this one.
  • Don't put "Buy", "Sell" or ticker symbols inside the clear space. HAKA PRO is a research tool, and the logo shouldn't sit next to trading calls.
